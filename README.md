---

## 🎯 Mission

Cloud Native UEMOA is a CNCF community chapter dedicated to promoting Cloud Native technologies
(Kubernetes, DevOps, Observability, Security, AI/ML, Open Source) across the eight UEMOA countries.

We aim to:

- Organize regular **meetups and workshops** (online & in-person),
- Share best practices around Kubernetes and the CNCF ecosystem,
- Support **sovereign cloud** and digital sovereignty initiatives in the region,
- Encourage **open source contributions** from local engineers,
- Prepare and host **Kubernetes Community Days UEMOA (KCD UEMOA)**.

All skill levels are welcome – from beginners to experts.

---

## 🌍 Countries Covered

- Benin  
- Burkina Faso  
- Côte d’Ivoire  
- Guinea-Bissau  
- Mali  
- Niger  
- Senegal  
- Togo
