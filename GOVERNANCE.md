# Cloud Native UEMOA – Governance

Cloud Native UEMOA is a volunteer-driven CNCF community chapter.

## Roles

- **Lead Organizer**  
  Coordinates the overall activity of the chapter, liaises with CNCF, and ensures alignment with CNCF guidelines.

- **Co-organizers**  
  Help plan and run meetups, manage communication, act as local points of contact in different UEMOA countries.

- **Contributors & Volunteers**  
  Support events, propose talks, prepare content, help with logistics and community outreach.

## Principles

- **Vendor-neutral**  
  Events and content must remain vendor-neutral and aligned with CNCF community policies.

- **Inclusive & Diverse**  
  We actively encourage diverse speakers, organizers and attendees across all UEMOA countries, backgrounds and experience levels.

- **Transparent**  
  Decisions regarding events, partnerships and programs are discussed openly, primarily through GitHub issues and community calls.

## Decision Making

- Day-to-day decisions are made by the Lead Organizer and co-organizers.
- Larger decisions (e.g., KCD UEMOA, partnerships) are discussed in:
  - Community calls,
  - GitHub issues,
  - With CNCF community staff when relevant.

## CNCF Alignment

This chapter operates under the CNCF Community Group guidelines and the CNCF Code of Conduct.

If you are interested in helping with governance or becoming a co-organizer, please open an issue in this repository.
